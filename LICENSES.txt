Thunder Player web TEST — audio licences
========================================

All clips used in this test harness are genuinely lossless-origin field
recordings (original WAV or FLAC). They were trimmed to 15–45 s excerpts,
converted to stereo 44.1 kHz / 16-bit, given a short fade in/out, and
peak-normalised (rumbles to about −4 dBFS, cracks to about −1 dBFS).
No lossy source (MP3, AAC, OGG/Vorbis) was used or upconverted.

The earlier thunder-player OGG bundle was not used.

Not used (and why)
------------------
- Existing /workspace/thunder-player/thunder-sounds.zip OGG files: lossy origin.
- BBC Sound Effects library: not freely licensed.
- Freesound downloads that require a login: not used; two Freesound-origin
  recordings above were taken from their Wikimedia Commons mirrors instead.
- NPS / NOAA sound library clips checked: the readily available downloads
  are MP3 (lossy origin), so they were skipped.
- CC BY-SA Commons recordings (for example Thunder crack 2B.wav,
  Very close thunder cracks.flac, the Groningen lightning WAVs) were
  available as lossless files but were not used, because this test pack
  is limited to public domain, CC0, or CC BY.
