Thunder Player web TEST — audio licences
========================================

All clips used in this test harness are genuinely lossless-origin field
recordings (original WAV or FLAC). They were trimmed to 15–45 s excerpts,
converted to stereo 44.1 kHz / 16-bit, given a short fade in/out, and
peak-normalised (rumbles to about −4 dBFS, cracks to about −1 dBFS).
No lossy source (MP3, AAC, OGG/Vorbis) was used or upconverted.

The earlier thunder-player OGG bundle was not used.

             the rumble-chunk bed have been removed. Rumble clips are still
             used as approach/recession thunder events.

Not used (and why)
------------------
- Existing /workspace/thunder-player/thunder-sounds.zip OGG files: lossy origin.
- BBC Sound Effects library: not freely licensed.
- Freesound downloads that require a login: not used; two Freesound-origin
  recordings above were taken from their Wikimedia Commons mirrors instead.
- NPS / NOAA sound library clips checked: the readily available downloads
  are MP3 (lossy origin), so they were skipped.
- CC BY-SA Commons recordings (for example Thunder crack 2B.wav,
  Very close thunder cracks.flac, the Groningen lightning WAVs) were
  available as lossless files but were not used, because this test pack
  is limited to public domain, CC0, or CC BY.

15. rain_thatch.flac and rain_wood.flac  — filtered rain beds   [added 19 Nov 2026]
    No freely licensed, lossless recording of rain on thatch or on a wooden
    roof (without thunder, voices or a lossy original) was found, so these
    two beds are EQ’d from the same Public Domain rain-on-leaves source as
    rain_bed.flac (raphael.malfliet, radio aporee / Internet Archive,
    https://archive.org/details/aporee_65214_75311). The unprocessed 34 s
    excerpt in source/aporee_65214_rain_on_leaves_excerpt_48k24.flac is the
    starting point.

    rain_thatch.flac: low-pass 7.5 kHz, high-shelf −3.5 dB at 5 kHz, low-shelf
    +1.5 dB at 180 Hz, then the same 2 s loop, 44.1 kHz / 16-bit, −4 dBFS.
    Intended to sound softer and more fibrous than open rain on leaves.

    rain_wood.flac: low-pass 5.5 kHz, peaking +3 dB at 280 Hz, high-shelf
    −5.5 dB at 4 kHz, then the same loop chain. Intended to sound duller
    and more wooden-bodied, with less leaf tick.

    Open-air listening still uses the unfiltered rain_bed.flac.
